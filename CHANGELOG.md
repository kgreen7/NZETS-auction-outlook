# Changelog

Changes to the published method. Each estimate in `outputs/auction_outlook_log.csv` records the model version used.

## auction-outlook 1.0 (12 Nov 2026)

First published version: 3-year empirical price range × reserve-clearing rate. Track record over 23 auctions (March 2021 to September 2026, each estimated about 12 weeks before the auction): Brier score 0.176 (lower is better). For comparison, two simple benchmarks score 0.280 (always forecasting the overall clearing rate) and 0.188 (the "is the price above the floor?" rule).
